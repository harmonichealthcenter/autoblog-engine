# Brand Comparison Article Type

These articles help a buyer compare Qi Coil with one named wellness-technology brand. The goal is a useful decision page, not an attack ad.

## Required approach

- Use the injected structured comparison source as the factual brief. Do not invent or silently update specifications.
- Compare the products on the dimensions buyers can verify: delivery method, form factor, program library, setup, portability, ownership model, published price, warranty or guarantee, and intended use.
- State clearly when the products solve different problems instead of forcing a false one-to-one comparison.
- Give the competitor its strongest fair case before explaining where Qi Coil differs.
- Attribute competitor claims to the competitor. Do not repeat medical or scientific claims as established fact.
- Treat legal allegations as allegations. Distinguish complaints and party statements from rulings. Never declare patent infringement without an adjudicated finding.
- Link naturally to the supplied Discover Qi Coil comparison page so the reader can inspect the underlying side-by-side brief.

## Structure

- 1,800 to 2,400 words.
- Open with the buyer's actual choice and the most important difference.
- Include a compact side-by-side table followed by prose that explains the tradeoffs.
- Include sections on technology, programs, setup and form factor, price and ownership, purchase protection, and who each option best fits.
- Close by inviting the reader to review the source comparison, patent information when relevant, and the Qi Coil guarantee. No hard-sell CTA.
